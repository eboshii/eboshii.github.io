I was attempting to build some artificial life simulations, training softbody cells to contract and relax muscles to propel themselves towards food in a physics environment, but found the cells would plateau pretty rapidly in their learning. 

There were limitations in my genetic algorithm and the physics environment that were preventing better learning, but it got me thinking about the factors in nature that prevent stagnation in evolution. 

- **Dynamic environments**. Changing amounts of food, changing temperatures, changing ph levels, changing chemical concentrations, etc, etc. 
- **Communities containing other creatures**. 
- **Brain-Body Co-Evolution**.
